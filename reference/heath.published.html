The Heath by Moonlight

Being a Night upon the Hampstead Road, in five Beats

A playable proof of concept. One Venture from a picaresque roguelike set in London, 1719–1745. Choices are gated by skills, possessions and reputation; risky ones go to Fortune's Wheel, with the odds shown first; consequences land at once, or arrive weeks later as rumours. Turn on the designer's view to see the machinery under each choice.
