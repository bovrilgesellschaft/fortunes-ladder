A Ball in Hanover Square

Being an Evening at Lady Bellaston's, to which our Heroine was not invited, in five Beats

A playable proof of concept, and a companion to The Heath by Moonlight. You play in the second person; whenever Fortune’s Wheel lands on Triumph or Catastrophe, the Story tab retells it as a third-person picaresque, after Smollett or after Sterne. One Venture from a picaresque roguelike set in London, 1719–1745, built on the same engine: choices gated by skills, possessions and reputation; risky ones sent to Fortune's Wheel with the odds shown first; consequences that land at once, or arrive later as rumours. Turn on the designer's view to see the machinery.
